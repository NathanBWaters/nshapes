NShapes

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 24, 2026 · Publisher: Nathan Waters

At a Glance

NShapes is a free, offline-first pattern-matching roguelike built by Nathan Waters. We believe games should respect your privacy: there are no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, and no cross-app tracking.

No Accounts or PII We never ask for your name, email, phone number, or location.
No Ads or Trackers No ad SDKs, no IDFA/GAID tracking, and no data sold to third parties.
Local-Only Saves Your campaign progress, unlocks, and settings stay on your device.
Opt-Out Analytics Anonymous gameplay balance telemetry can be turned off anytime in Options.

1. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

NShapes stores your game progress (Adventure stars, unlocked fighters, weapons, Arcana, Starfall Summit runs, Prism Deck runs, battle history, and audio/accessibility preferences) locally on your device using on-device storage. This data is never uploaded to a player account server.

You can erase all locally stored game progress at any time via Options → Reset Game, or by uninstalling the app.

2. Anonymous Gameplay Analytics (PostHog)

To balance weapons, enemies, and difficulty across updates, NShapes sends lightweight, anonymous gameplay telemetry to PostHog (hosted in the United States at us.i.posthog.com):

  • Events sent: One session-open event (app_opened) and one summary event per finished fight or round (fight_ended), including optional thumbs-up/thumbs-down fun ratings if you choose to tap them.
  • What is included: Random anonymous install identifier (not linked to your identity, Apple ID, or Google account), app version, platform (iOS / Android / Web), selected fighter/weapons/level, match outcome, and fight balance statistics.
  • What is NOT collected: No names, emails, contacts, precise location, advertising identifiers (IDFA/GAID), photos, microphone audio, or cross-app activity.

How to opt out: Open Options from the Main Menu and toggle Gameplay Analytics to OFF. When disabled, NShapes stops sending analytics events immediately and discards any unsent local analytics queue.

3. Platform Crash Diagnostics & App Updates

On iOS and Android, Apple and Google may provide anonymized crash logs and performance diagnostics if you have opted into diagnostics in your device's system settings. NShapes also uses Expo Updates (EAS Update) to check for over-the-air JavaScript game updates using only standard HTTP metadata (app version and platform).

4. External Links

NShapes includes optional links to our community Discord server and support pages. Visiting an external website or Discord is governed by that service's own privacy policy.

5. Children's Privacy

NShapes is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe any information has been inadvertently collected, please reach out via our support channels below so we can address it promptly.

6. Changes & Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy as NShapes evolves. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Questions or privacy requests? Reach Nathan Waters via GitHub Issues or the NShapes Discord, or visit our Support Page.
