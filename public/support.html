Frequently Asked Questions

How do I turn on Colorblind Mode?

NShapes' default card palette (Tangerine Orange, Electric Blue, and Berry Pink with Circle, Triangle, and Bolt shapes) is engineered to be colorblind-safe out of the box. For extra clarity or complete achromatopsia, open Options from the Main Menu and toggle Colorblind Mode to ON to add crisp O / B / P corner badges to every card.

I don't hear sound or music on iPhone.

Check two things: (1) open Options and ensure Music and Sound Effects are toggled ON, and (2) check your iPhone's side Silent / Ring switch — NShapes respects iOS silent mode so you can play quietly or alongside background music and podcasts.

How do I opt out of Gameplay Analytics?

Open Options and switch Gameplay Analytics to OFF. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

How do I reset my save and start fresh?

Open Options → Reset Game and confirm START OVER. This permanently erases all local Adventure stars, unlocks, Starfall Summit runs, Prism Deck runs, and battle history on your device.

Does NShapes work offline?

Yes! All four modes — Adventure, Starfall Summit, Prism Deck, and Free Play — run 100% offline with local device saves.
